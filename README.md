# ITS: TH Köln – Power Quality Management of Gummersbach Campus

Case study at TH Köln, winter term 2026/27.

We build a system that collects data from the energy meters on the
Gummersbach campus, stores it centrally, checks power quality against
EN 50160, and shows the results on dashboards for PC and phone.
